Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how the current version of MinuteRush handles information when you play its browser games.

Information stored in your browser

MinuteRush uses your browser's local storage to save game counts and personal-best scores. This information stays in the browser profile on your device. It is not currently sent to a MinuteRush server, and there is no global leaderboard in this version.

Contact form

The contact page prepares an email using your device's email application. MinuteRush does not receive the message through a website server. Your email application and provider may handle it under their own privacy policies. The contact form must be configured with the site owner's real email address before use.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current version does not intentionally include third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or advertising cookies. If these services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to explain what data they collect and how users can manage their choices before those services are enabled.

Children and personal information

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through feedback messages. The games do not require an account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MinuteRush changes. The date at the top should be updated whenever a material change is made.

Questions

For privacy questions, visit the Contact page. The site owner should configure a working contact address before launch.
