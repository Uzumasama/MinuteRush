Quick Tap: A Guide to the 60-Second Reflex Challenge

Quick Tap is the simplest game on MinuteRush: a target appears, and each successful tap adds one point. Your round lasts 60 seconds. The goal is to see how many taps you can make before the timer reaches zero.

Quick rules
  • Start the 60-second round.
  • Tap the target whenever it is available.
  • Each successful tap earns one point.
  • When time runs out, your score and personal-best result appear.

How to approach a round

Find a comfortable position before starting. On a phone or tablet, hold the device steadily and use the finger that lets you tap comfortably. On a desktop, place your pointer near the target. You do not need to press harder; a normal, deliberate tap is enough.

Try to keep a steady rhythm instead of making tense, uncontrolled movements. If you miss or your pace slows, simply return your attention to the target. There is no penalty for taking a brief moment to regain a comfortable rhythm.

Common things that can affect your score

  • Device position: an awkward grip can make repeated taps less comfortable.
  • Accidental touches: keep other fingers away from the play area.
  • Rushing: very hurried movements can lead to missed taps or discomfort.
  • Distractions: notifications and switching apps can interrupt your round.

How to use your personal best

Your best score is a simple record of your own previous round on that browser. Play again and compare your result with that record. A lower score does not mean you have failed; pace can vary with attention, device, and circumstances.

Does Quick Tap measure reaction time?

Quick Tap is a casual tapping challenge, not a calibrated reaction-time test. Its score counts successful taps over a minute. It should not be treated as a medical, scientific, or professional measurement of reflexes.
