Three quick games. One minute at a time.

MinuteRush is a free collection of small browser games made for short breaks. Choose a challenge, play for 60 seconds, and try to beat your own best score. The games run on this page, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

Quick Tap — a reflex challenge

Tap the target as often as you can before the 60-second timer ends. Each successful tap adds one point. It is a simple way to test how quickly you can respond.

Read the Quick Tap guide →

Memory Grid — a visual memory game

Watch the highlighted tiles, then select the positions you remember. Complete a pattern to move on to a harder round. Incorrect choices reduce your score.

Read the Memory Grid guide →

Number Rush — a number sequence game

Look at the shuffled numbers and select them from smallest to largest. Correct selections earn points, while mistakes cost a point.

Read the Number Rush guide →

Not sure which challenge fits your break? Compare the three games and choose where to start →

How to play MinuteRush

  1. Choose Quick Tap, Memory Grid, or Number Rush above.
  2. Read the short instructions and start the 60-second round.
  3. Watch your score as you play. When time runs out, your result is shown.
  4. Play again whenever you want to improve your personal best.

Frequently asked questions

Are the MinuteRush games free?

Yes. The games on this website are free to play in your browser.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. Open the website in a modern browser and choose a game. No app installation or account is required.

Where are my scores saved?

Your personal bests and game count are saved in local storage in the browser on your device. They are not currently synced between devices or shared on a public leaderboard.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. MinuteRush is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.
