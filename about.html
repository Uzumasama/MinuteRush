About MinuteRush

MinuteRush is a small browser-game project built around one simple idea: a short break can be fun, playful, and a little challenging.

Play quick rounds, test your reflexes, train your memory, and practice number sequencing. The games are designed to work directly in your browser, without requiring a download or account.

Our mini-games

  • Quick Tap: tap the target as many times as you can in 60 seconds.
  • Memory Grid: remember the glowing tiles and find them again.
  • Number Rush: select numbers from smallest to largest.

Your personal bests and game count are stored locally in your browser on this device. They are not currently shared as a global leaderboard.

MinuteRush is an independent project. It is not intended to provide medical, educational, or professional advice.
