About MinuteRush

MinuteRush is a small browser-game project built around one simple idea: a short break can be fun, playful, and a little challenging.

Play quick rounds, test your reflexes, train your memory, and practice number sequencing. The games are designed to work directly in your browser, without requiring a download or account.

Our mini-games

  • Quick Tap: tap the target as many times as you can in 60 seconds.
  • Memory Grid: remember the glowing tiles and find them again.
  • Number Rush: select numbers from smallest to largest.

Designed for short, simple sessions

Each challenge is built around a one-minute round. You can choose a game based on the kind of activity you feel like doing: repeated tapping, remembering positions, or ordering numbers. There is no need to finish a course, unlock an account, or compete against strangers.

How scores and progress work

MinuteRush keeps personal-best scores and the number of games played in your browser's local storage. This means the information is tied to that browser profile. It may not appear if you switch browsers, use another device, clear site data, or browse in private mode. The current version does not sync scores between devices and does not provide a public leaderboard.

Play at your own pace

The games are intended as casual entertainment. A score can change from one round to another depending on the device, screen size, attention, and the moment you play. You do not need to treat a result as a measure of your ability.

Learn the games

  • Quick Tap guide — rules and ways to approach the 60-second tapping round.
  • Memory Grid guide — how to remember and select the highlighted tiles.
  • Number Rush guide — how to select five numbers in ascending order.

MinuteRush is an independent project. It is not intended to provide medical, educational, or professional advice.
