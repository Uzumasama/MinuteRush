A MinuteRush player guide

Which MinuteRush Game Should You Play?

Not every short break calls for the same kind of challenge. Sometimes you want to tap quickly; sometimes you would rather remember a pattern or work through a small sequence. MinuteRush has three different mini-games, each built around a simple 60-second round.

Choose by the kind of challenge you want

🎯 Quick Tap: choose a fast, repeatable challenge

Quick Tap is the most direct of the three games. A target appears and you tap it as often as possible before the timer ends. Each successful tap adds one point.

Try it when you want a simple task with an immediate response. You do not need to remember a sequence or compare several options; the challenge is to keep a steady rhythm for the full round.

Read the Quick Tap rules and tips →

🧠 Memory Grid: choose a visual recall challenge

Memory Grid shows a group of glowing positions on a 3-by-3 board. After the highlights disappear, select the positions you remember. Correct choices add points, while an incorrect choice reduces your score.

Try it when you feel like paying attention to positions and patterns. One simple approach is to scan the board in a consistent order, such as left to right and top to bottom.

Read the Memory Grid rules and tips →

🔢 Number Rush: choose an ordering challenge

Number Rush presents five shuffled numbers. Select them from smallest to largest. A correct selection earns one point; a wrong selection costs one point.

Try it when you want to compare values and decide what comes next. Scanning all five numbers before making your first selection can help you form an order.

Read the Number Rush rules and tips →

A simple way to pick

  • Want a straightforward tapping task? Start with Quick Tap.
  • Want to remember where things appeared? Choose Memory Grid.
  • Want to compare and arrange values? Choose Number Rush.

How to use your score

Your score records how a particular round went; it is not a formal test of reflexes, memory, intelligence, or mathematical ability. Results can vary with your device, browser, screen, attention, and interruptions. Compare your rounds as a personal game challenge, not as a judgment about yourself.

Play without setting anything up

All three games run directly in your browser. No download or account is required. Personal-best scores and game counts are stored locally in your browser profile, so they are not automatically shared between devices.

When you are ready, return to the MinuteRush homepage and choose a one-minute challenge.
